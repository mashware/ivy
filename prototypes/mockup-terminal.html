What to look at in the panel

The column of marks on the left is what lets you scan down without reading. With four splits and a narrow panel, check whether it still works or whether lines have to be cut.

Context, cost and connections

The one thing only the agent can know. The bar turns amber at 50% and orange at 75%, to see at a glance which one is close to compacting. MT-15863 is at 76% and has GitLab down.

Working or waiting for you

The dot next to the ticket pulses while the agent is working and goes dark when it is waiting for you. With four splits it is what tells you where to look without reading anything.

Folding per split

Each panel's ▸ shrinks it to a strip with only the indicator. Try folding two and leaving one open: it is the real mode when you work in one and keep an eye on the rest.

The buttons are the new part

Each one writes into the terminal of its own split. The producer declares them in the state file with send; the app only paints them and fires them.
